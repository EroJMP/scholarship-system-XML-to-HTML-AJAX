Frequently Asked Questions

Account Related Questions

Managing your account is important for a smooth scholarship application experience. Below are answers to common issues like forgotten passwords, locked accounts, and email confirmation problems. Make sure your login credentials are secure and your account details are always updated.

1. How do I recover my account if I forgot my password?

If you forgot your password, click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email address to receive a password reset link. Follow the instructions in the email to set a new password.

2. Why is my account locked?

Your account may be temporarily locked after multiple failed login attempts for security purposes. Wait for 30 minutes before trying again. If the issue persists, contact support at scholarship@pasigcity.gov.ph.

3. I didn't receive the email confirmation. What should I do?

First, check your Spam or Junk folder. If it's not there, wait a few minutes and try resending the confirmation from the login page. If the issue continues, reach out to the support team for help.

4. Can I change the password linked to my account?

Yes, go to Account Settings after logging in. You can change your password there. Make sure to save changes.

5. Can I update the information I submitted when creating my account?

Yes, you can update your personal information if you made a mistake, such as a wrong spelling or incorrect detail. Just log in to your account, go to Account Settings, and make the necessary changes. Don’t forget to save your updates before exiting.

Requirements Related Questions

Submitting the correct documents is essential for your scholarship application to be processed. Below are answers to common questions about uploading, updating, and verifying your requirements. Make sure all submitted documents are complete, accurate, and within the deadline.

1. Do I need to submit hard copies of the documents?

Yes, if specified. While initial submission is done online, hard copy submission might still be required. Always check the Announcements section for hard copy deadlines and submission instructions.

2. How do I update or correct a document I already submitted?

You can update or replace your submitted documents anytime before the deadline through the dashboard under the 'Application' or 'Documents' section.

3. Can I submit requirements after the deadline?

Late submissions are generally not accepted. Make sure to submit all requirements on or before the deadline to avoid disqualification.

4. How will I know if my submitted documents are accepted?

You will receive updates on your application status through your dashboard and email notifications regarding the acceptance or need for corrections of your documents.

5. Can I submit documents on behalf of someone else?

Only the applicant can submit documents to ensure authenticity and privacy. If you need assistance, contact support for guidance.

Application Related Questions

Navigating the scholarship application process can be straightforward when you know the steps. Below are answers to common questions about starting your application, submitting documents, checking status, and what to expect during the review. Follow each step carefully to improve your chances of success.

1. How do I start my scholarship application?

Begin by creating an account on the portal, then complete your profile with accurate personal and academic information before browsing and applying for scholarships.

2. What if I make a mistake during the application? Can I edit my submission?

You can update or correct your application details and documents before the deadline via your dashboard in the 'Application' section.

3. How long does the application review process take?

Review times vary but typically take 2 to 4 weeks after the deadline. You will receive updates via email and through the portal dashboard.

4. Can I withdraw my application once submitted?

Yes, you can withdraw your application before the deadline through your Account Settings if you decide not to pursue the scholarship.

5. How do I check my application status?

Log in to your account and visit the 'Dashboard' section to see real-time updates on your application status.

6. What should I do if I didn’t receive any updates after applying?

Check your spam/junk email folder and ensure your contact details are correct. If you still don’t receive updates, contact support at scholarship@pasigcity.gov.ph.

How to Contact Us?

If you encounter any issues or have questions not answered in the portal, don't worry — help is available. Below are common inquiries about reaching our support team, response time, and where to send your concerns. Clear communication ensures faster assistance.

1. How can I contact the IskolarLinkPasig support team?

You can reach us by sending an email to scholarship@pasigcity.gov.ph. Please include your full name, student number, and a brief description of your concern for faster assistance.

2. What should I do if I don't receive a reply?

Please allow up to 3-5 working days for a response. If you don't receive a reply within that time, you may follow up with another email, making sure to include your previous message for reference.

3. Can I call or visit the office for support?

At the moment, support is only available through email to streamline and document all concerns properly. For official announcements about in-person assistance, check the Announcements section on the portal.

User Application Guide

This guide walks you through every step of using the IskolarLinkPasig Scholarship Portal — from creating an account to applying for scholarships and managing your profile. Follow the instructions below to navigate the system smoothly and make the most out of your scholarship opportunities.

Account Creation Process

Creating your account is the first step to accessing the IskolarLink Scholarship Portal. Make sure all information you enter is accurate, as this will be used throughout your scholarship application process.

Steps

1. Go to the portal homepage and click on the “Register” button at the top-right corner.

2. Fill in the registration form completely. All fields that have "*" are required.

3. Set a strong password that includes at least 8 characters, one number, and one special symbol.

4. Double-check your personal details for any typos or incorrect entries.

5. Submit the form. You'll receive a confirmation email with a verification link. Click the link in your email to verify your account and complete the registration. Log in using your credentials and start setting up your profile.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your main control panel once you're logged in to the IskolarLinkPasig Scholarship Portal. It provides a quick overview of your application status, profile details, available scholarships, and important updates. Knowing how to navigate your dashboard helps you stay informed and on track throughout the application process.

1. Application Status

See the current stage of your application—whether it's submitted, under review, approved, or declined. You'll also be notified here if further action is needed.

2. Submitted Applications

View a summary of the scholarships you've applied for, including application date and current status.

Application Process

Applying for a scholarship through the IskolarLinkPasig Scholarship Portal is a guided and user-friendly experience. To ensure your application is successfully submitted and considered, follow the steps below carefully. Make sure all your information and documents are accurate and complete.

Steps to Apply for a Scholarship:
1. Log In to Your Account

Use your registered email and password to access the IskolarLinkPasig Portal.

2. Get Started

Click the "Apply Now" button in the dashboard to get started. You will then be directed to the list of scholarship programs offered by Pasig City.

3. Browse through the list of Scholarship Programs Offered

Choose the program you want to apply for. Click the "View Details" button next to your selected scholarship to view complete information about the program. You’ll be able to see a breakdown of the requirements you need to prepare, such as personal documents, academic records, and proof of residency. The eligibility section outlines who can apply based on residency, academic performance, and family income. You’ll also find details about the benefits offered—such as tuition coverage, monthly allowances, and book support—as well as the total scholarship amount and the deadline for submitting your application.

4. Click "Apply Now" to Proceed

You will now be directed to the specific scholarship program you selected, where you will see an application form that you need to fill out completely and accurately.

5. Once done, review your form and click "Submit" to apply.

You may still update your application details before the deadline, so make sure everything is accurate and complete.

Account Settings

Keeping your account information up to date ensures smooth communication and accurate application processing. Through the Account Settings section, you can manage personal details, change your password, and adjust notification preferences.

1. Updating Personal Information

You can update your name, contact details, or other personal information you provided when creating your account. To do this, go to Account Settings, click "Edit" next to the information you want to change, make the correction, and save your changes.

Reminder: Changes here do not affect your submitted scholarship application form. To update your application details, please go to the dashboard and click "View Submitted Form" — you can still edit it before the deadline. After the deadline, no further changes are allowed.

2. Changing Your Password

For security, regularly update your password. Go to Account Settings, select "Change Password", enter your current password and your new password, then save.
