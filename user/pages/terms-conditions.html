Terms and Conditions

Introduction

Welcome to IskolarLink, the official scholarship portal of Pasig City. This Terms and Conditions page outlines how we manage your personal information and digital interactions within the platform. At IskolarLink, we uphold the highest standards of transparency, accountability, and lawful data processing, in strict alignment with the Republic Act No. 10173 or the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

By accessing or using this portal, users signify their understanding and agreement to the policies described herein. We take the responsibility of protecting your data very seriously, and we encourage every applicant, guardian, and stakeholder to read through these terms carefully.

Privacy Principles

IskolarLink operates on a set of core privacy principles to guide our data practices. We commit to transparency by clearly informing users about what data is collected and how it is used. Our data collection is always rooted in legitimate, lawful, and justifiable purposes, especially in the context of scholarship applications and administration.

We uphold proportionality by collecting only the necessary data relevant to scholarship qualification. Our systems are equipped with technical and organizational safeguards to ensure the security of your information. Lastly, we take full accountability for all data handling processes and are committed to complying with applicable laws and regulations.

Data We Collect

To effectively process and evaluate scholarship applications, IskolarLink collects various categories of personal data. These include your full legal name, date and place of birth, contact details (such as phone number, email address, and home address), academic history, family income, and other socioeconomic indicators. Information regarding your parent or legal guardian may also be collected.

Additionally, we may require the upload of supporting documents including government-issued IDs, academic transcripts, barangay certifications, and proofs of residency. Login credentials and activity logs are also generated to ensure portal security and accountability.

Purpose of Collection

The data we collect through IskolarLink serves several essential purposes. Primarily, it enables us to verify applicant eligibility, assess academic performance, and evaluate financial need. These criteria are vital to ensuring that scholarships are awarded fairly and to those who need them most.

We also use your data to communicate status updates, generate internal reports for transparency and policy improvement, and comply with government-mandated reporting. Your information helps us design better user experiences and enhances the overall efficiency of the scholarship program.

Data Privacy

IskolarLink is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. This section outlines our policies and responsibilities when handling sensitive information, particularly with regard to children’s data and other supporting details. We aim to ensure that your data is processed lawfully, securely, and with utmost respect for your rights as a data subject.

Children's Data

We recognize the importance of protecting the privacy and data of minors. For applicants below the age of 18, we require verifiable consent from a parent or legal guardian before collecting any information. We implement extra measures to ensure that the personal data of minors is handled with the highest level of care, confidentiality, and legal compliance.

Additional Privacy Information

Personal data submitted to IskolarLink is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose of collection or as mandated by applicable laws and audit requirements. We do not sell, lease, or use your information for any purpose other than those explicitly stated in this policy.

In certain cases, we may share your data with relevant government agencies or authorized personnel strictly for educational, monitoring, or legal purposes. You have the right to access your data, request corrections, restrict certain processing activities, or ask for your data to be deleted — subject to approval and verification.

We maintain strong security practices such as encrypted transmissions, secure servers, and access control systems to guard against unauthorized use or disclosure of data. This policy is subject to updates as we continue to improve our platform and comply with new legal frameworks. All revisions will be reflected on this page.
