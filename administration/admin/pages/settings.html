Deadline Reminder: College Scholarship Applications
Submission deadline for scholarship applications is August 15, 2025.

This is a reminder that the deadline for College Scholarship applications is August 15, 2023. All interested applicants are advised to prepare their requirements ahead of time to avoid delays. Documents to be submitted include a duly accomplished application form, recent 2x2 ID photo, latest school ID, certificate of registration or enrollment, and a copy of grades from the previous semester. Applicants must also provide proof of residency within the city, barangay clearance, and a certificate of indigency if applicable. Submission of falsified documents will result in disqualification. Applications can be submitted online through our official portal or in person at the Office of the City Scholarship Program located at 2nd Floor, City Hall Annex Building. The office is open from Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Please ensure that all scanned documents for online submission are clear and legible. Failure to comply with the requirements or missing documents may delay the processing of your application. Notification of qualified applicants will be posted on the official website and social media pages. For inquiries, you may contact (02) 8123-4567 or email scholarships@pasigcity.gov.ph. We highly encourage all eligible students to apply and take advantage of this opportunity to receive financial support for your education. Let us continue working together to ensure access to quality education for all Pasigueños.

Event
Scholarship Awarding Ceremony
All approved scholars are invited to attend the Scholarship Awarding Ceremony on September 1, 2023

All approved scholars are invited to attend the Scholarship Awarding Ceremony on September 1, 2023, at the Pasig City Convention Center. Registration starts at 8:00 AM and the program will begin at 9:00 AM. Attendees are advised to wear smart casual attire. The event will be graced by key officials from the City Government and representatives from our partner institutions. Scholars will receive their certificates and scholarship cards during the event. Parents or guardians are welcome to attend. Light snacks and refreshments will be provided. There will also be an orientation on scholarship guidelines, responsibilities of scholars, and updates on new features in the online portal. Attendance is mandatory. Those who cannot attend due to valid reasons are required to coordinate with the scholarship office at least five working days before the event. Let us celebrate this milestone together as we continue to promote academic excellence and inclusive opportunities for all Pasigueño scholars.

New Educational Assistance Program
The City Government of Pasig is proud to announce a new Educational Assistance Program

The City Government of Pasig is proud to announce the launch of a new Educational Assistance Program aimed at supporting students from low-income families. This program will provide financial aid to help cover school-related expenses such as transportation, school supplies, and internet connectivity. Qualified beneficiaries will receive a monthly allowance depending on their level of study—elementary, high school, or college. Priority will be given to those residing in underserved barangays. Interested applicants must submit proof of income, a barangay endorsement, a certified copy of grades, and a personal letter of intent explaining how this assistance will help their studies. Applications will be accepted starting May 1, 2025. Orientation seminars will be conducted in various barangays to inform families about the program and how to apply. This initiative reflects our continued commitment to closing the education gap and ensuring no Pasigueño student is left behind due to financial hardship.

Scholarship Renewal Notice for Ongoing Scholars
The City Government of Pasig is proud to announce a new Educational Assistance Program

All current scholars are reminded to renew their scholarship status before the upcoming semester. Renewal requirements include submission of updated grades, current enrollment certificate, and a short performance evaluation signed by a school official. The deadline for submission is July 31, 2025. Scholars must maintain the required General Weighted Average (GWA) to remain eligible. Those who fall below the minimum GWA will be placed under probation or may lose their scholarship privileges depending on the number of offenses and reasons cited. Justification letters may be submitted for evaluation in case of special circumstances. Renewals may be submitted online or in person. Ensure your documents are complete and accurate to avoid processing delays. The scholarship office will not accept late or incomplete submissions. Updates on the status of your renewal will be sent via email. For assistance, please visit our website or call our hotline. Let us all remain committed to academic excellence and responsibility. Your performance and dedication make this program successful for generations to come.
